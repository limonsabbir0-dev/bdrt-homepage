About BDRT

Built to hold.
Built to grow.

BDRT Holdings is a Vallejo-based holding company. We build, back and grow ventures across media, advertising, construction, education and emerging brands — each one held to the same standard: clear vision, disciplined execution, lasting value.

History

From one vision
to a portfolio.

BDRT began with a simple belief: culture and commerce are not opposites — they amplify each other. What started as a single creative ambition became a house of ventures. Today, iMERGE Advertising, Involia, Academic Elements, VOXA House and VOXA Brand each carry that belief into a different field.

What we believe

  • Bold ideas, executed with precision.
  • Commerce with an editorial eye.
  • Growth that outlasts a campaign.
